Project Overview

The Problem: Early warning signs of churn often get buried in product telemetry or isolated CSM gut feel, leading to delayed account interventions.

The Solution: Built a health scoring engine across 60 accounts that compares data-driven health metrics against qualitative CSM sentiment. The tool flags disconnects and auto-generates drafted outreach for account leads to review.
