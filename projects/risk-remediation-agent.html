AI Account Risk Remediation Agent

Project Overview

The Problem: Customer health dashboards flag *when* an account is at risk, but Customer Success Managers (CSMs) and Operations leaders still spend hours manually triaging root causes, summarizing risks for executives, and drafting recovery playbooks.

The Solution: This interactive AI agent ingests account telemetry (ARR, usage drops, unresolved tickets, renewal timelines, CSAT) and generates a complete 3-part retention package in seconds: an internal executive briefing, an empathetic CSM re-engagement email, and a 4-week turnaround milestone plan.

Business Impact: Demonstrates how AI automation scales account management capacity, standardizes risk interventions across teams, and protects recurring revenue before renewal conversations begin.
