Project Overview

The Problem: System migrations and enterprise rollouts generate vast amounts of unstructured feedback when timelines slip. Program managers spend hours manually categorizing feedback and aligning teams on corrective actions.

The Solution: This AI tool ingests raw meeting notes and team retrospectives, mapping friction points into a cross-functional root cause matrix, quantifying SLA timeline impact, and outputting actionable 30-60-90 day remediation roadmaps.

Business Impact: Eliminates manual retro synthesis, establishes operational accountability across departments, and ensures recurring migration errors are fixed permanently.
